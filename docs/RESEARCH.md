# Sahra menu: culinary and visual direction

Research date: 28 October 2026. This is a fictional restaurant; recipes and USD prices are invented for the concept, informed by the sources below. They are not copied restaurant recipes or offers.

- https://www.mandihouse.com/menu — Primary restaurant menu: structure, mandi, haneeth, madhbi, fasolia, shakshuka, saltah, fahsa, breads and sides. Used to establish category breadth, not copy descriptions or prices.
- https://yemenipalace.com/menu — Primary restaurant menu: tandoor fish, rice plates, family platters, sahawiq with cheese, and fresh breads. Used for menu coverage and serving ideas.
- https://asif.org/en/recipes/shafoot/ — Attributed recipe for lahoh with yogurt, garlic, chilli, scallion, cilantro and mint. Informed the shafoot concept.
- https://www.shebayemenifood.com/content/bint-al-sahn — Recipe by Sheba Yemeni Food: thin layers of wheat dough with egg, ghee, honey and black seeds. Informed dessert description and photo art direction.
- https://qishrcoffeehouse.co/ — Primary café source for qishr and Adeni tea. Informed drinks category.
- https://www.worldtravelguide.net/guides/middle-east/yemen/food-and-drink/ — Secondary cross-check for saltah, fahsa, fasolia, bint al sahn and coffee-husk qishr.

Visual research: image search for Yemeni mandi, haneeth, saltah and shafoot. Third-party image results used for subject research only; no third-party photography was copied. Six original food photographs generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Prompts recorded in IMAGE-PROMPTS.md.

Design: menu-only, saffron-yellow / charcoal / warm paper, condensed bold display type, bilingual names, rice and fire → stone pots → small plates → bread → sweets → drinks. No landing-page story, booking, contact or video sections.

The user's frontend-design skill informs bold typography, layout and motion. Their newskill template informs plain HTML/CSS/vanilla JS and CDN fonts. Video-to-website is deferred per the user's instruction. Their first SKILL.md is a cold-email workflow unrelated to this task.
